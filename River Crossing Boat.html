Understanding Relative Velocity

Adjust the controls to see how the boat's direction and river current combine to determine its path.
Velocity of boat relative to ground:
vBG = vBW + vWG

Across-river velocity:
vx = vboat cos(θ)

Downstream velocity:
vy = vcurrent + vboat sin(θ)

Crossing time:
t = River Width / vx

Downstream drift:
d = vy × t
